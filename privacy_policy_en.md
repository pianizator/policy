## Summary

flat pool is an offline single-player game. We do not create accounts, show ads, or run analytics SDKs in the App. The App does not require an internet connection to play. We do not collect personal information from you through the App.

---

## Information stored on your device

The App stores the following data **only on your device**:

- Game settings (for example: swipe sensitivity, aim helpers, pocket size, mute, table rotation preference)
- Whether the how-to-play introduction has been shown
- Your top score
- An unfinished game session, so you can continue later

This data is used solely to run the game on your device. It is not uploaded to our servers — we do not operate game servers for flat pool.

---

## What we do not collect

Through the App we do not collect, and we do not ask you to provide:

- Name, email, phone number, or postal address
- Precise or approximate location
- Photos, contacts, or files from your device (beyond the App’s own save data)
- Advertising IDs for tracking
- Payment or financial information (the App has no in-app purchases)

The App’s Android package does not declare the `INTERNET` permission for its own networking features.

## Ads, analytics, and third-party SDKs

The App does not include advertising networks, crash reporters, or analytics SDKs that would send your data to third parties for marketing or measurement.

## Android backup and device transfer

If Android Auto Backup / cloud backup or device-to-device transfer is enabled on your device and for this App, Android (Google) may back up App data (such as settings, top score, and a saved game) to your Google account or transfer it to another device according to your system settings and Google’s policies. That process is controlled by Android and your Google account, not by a separate service we run. You can manage backup in your device settings.
