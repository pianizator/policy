Privacy Policy 5/8 Code Breaker Effective date: 11 November 2026 This policy describes how the Android app 5/8 Code Breaker handles information. The app is a puzzle game that runs on your device. Information stored on your device The app saves your high score in the app’s private storage on the device. That score is used only to show “TOP” in the game. It is not sent to the developer and it is not sent to any server. The current game, including the secret code and the score of the session you are playing, stays in memory while the app is open. It is not written to a server. Information we do not collect The app does not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise or approximate location, photos, contacts, or advertising identifiers. It has no account system. It does not show ads and it does not include analytics or tracking tools. It does not request internet access, so it cannot transmit data to the developer. Backups Android may include the app’s local data, including the high score, in a backup of your device if you have device backup turned on. That backup is handled by Google as part of your device backup. The developer does not receive it. You can turn device backup off in Android settings. Children The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. How long information stays The high score remains on the device until you clear the app’s storage or uninstall the app. Uninstalling the app removes its local data from the device. Changes If this policy changes, the new text will be posted at this page with a new effective date.
